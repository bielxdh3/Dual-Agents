# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Dual Agents should be a productive technical space. Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate with respect, focus criticism on the work, and make room for disagreement without personal attacks.

Acceptable participation includes:

- constructive technical feedback;
- clear disagreement backed by evidence;
- asking questions in good faith;
- respecting privacy and security boundaries;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, or targeted abuse;
- discriminatory or demeaning language;
- publishing another person's private information;
- deliberate disruption, spam, or bad-faith participation;
- pressuring contributors to disclose credentials, private data, or security-sensitive details.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions related to the project, and other public project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content; close contributions; restrict participation; or take other proportionate action when behavior harms the project or its participants.

For ordinary moderation concerns, use the repository's public issue or pull-request context when appropriate. For a sensitive report that should not be public, contact the maintainer through a private contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile.

Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not this process.

## Good-faith enforcement

Enforcement should consider context, severity, repetition, impact, and whether the participant made a good-faith effort to correct the behavior.
